[Social]
No real life politics.

Treat other players respectfully.

[Ships]

[Stations]
Mass limit: 500k

Homebase mass limit: 1.3m

[Planets]
Do **not** make homebases on planets.

Do **not** mine planets.

Do **not** blow up planets.

Planets are used for passive resource income. Use */guide* in-game for more info.

Do **not** use alternative accounts to create invulnerable homebase extractors. This is a bannable offence.

[Fleets]
Fleets cannot have more than 20 total members and 60 total entities across all members.

Fleets cannot exceed a combined total mass of 500k.

Keep fleets to 1 per sector per faction, and never in planet sectors.

[Other]
Do not damage or modify server infrastructure such as public warpgates.

Any and all exploits will result in both you and your faction receiving permanent bans.
